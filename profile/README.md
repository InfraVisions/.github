## 🌐 About Infra Visions

**Infra Visions** is an AI-driven Smart Cities and Geospatial Information Systems (GIS) SaaS platform built to digitize, monitor, and optimize physical infrastructure at scale. 

We eliminate fragmented inspection workflows by unifying aerial, thermal, LiDAR, and ground telemetry into a single cloud-native intelligence layer—empowering utility operators, municipalities, engineering firms, and commercial drone service providers to transition from reactive repairs to predictive maintenance.

---

## 🚀 Core Platform Pillars

### 1. 🔍 AI Asset & Thermal Inspection
Automated computer vision and thermal diagnostic pipelines that detect, classify, and georeference structural and thermal anomalies with compliance-ready reporting:
- **Solar PV Farms:** Hotspot detection, string outages, cell degradation, and soiling analysis (IEC TS 62446-3 aligned).
- **Wind Turbines:** Automated blade surface erosion, lightning strike, and structural crack detection.
- **Power Grids & Telecom Towers:** 3D power line corridor mapping, vegetation encroachment alerts, corrosion checks, and insulator diagnostics.
- **Civil & Industrial Assets:** Automated road distress classification (cracks, potholes) and thermal facility inspections.

### 2. 🗺️ 3D Mapping, GIS & Digital Twins
Survey-grade spatial computing that transforms raw sensor captures into living, interactive 3D replicas of physical assets:
- **Infrastructure Digital Twins:** Dynamic 3D environments tracking asset health and degradation over time.
- **Photogrammetry & LiDAR:** High-resolution orthomosaics, dense 3D point clouds, and LiDAR 3D structural modeling.
- **Automated Feature Extraction:** Building footprints, road networks, and forestry/vegetation analysis.

### 3. 🚁 Drone Operator Command Dashboard
A turnkey B2B SaaS workspace engineered for commercial drone service providers to scale operations:
- **Mission & Fleet Management:** End-to-end flight tracking, payload ingestion, and mission organization.
- **Automated AI Pipelines:** Zero-touch cloud processing from raw RGB/Thermal/LiDAR uploads to actionable analytics.
- **White-Label Client Delivery:** Share interactive maps, 3D models, and inspection reports with enterprise clients through a branded portal.
